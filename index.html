Meet KICKS ↗
Do I need an account to explore KICKS?+

You can browse fields, games, and tournaments before signing in. An account is needed to book or join.

How do I book a football field?+

Choose a field, check its details, and select an available time to make your booking.

Can I join without having a full team?+

Yes. Open Games lets you join a match as an individual player while places are available.

How do tournament entries work?+

Open a tournament to see its format, entry fee, schedule, and registration details.

Can I list my own football field?+

Field owners can sign in to manage their fields and publish their details.
