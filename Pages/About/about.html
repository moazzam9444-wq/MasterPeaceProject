WHY KICKS EXISTS

Football is better
when everyone can join.

Finding a place to play can take too much time. KICKS brings fields, bookings, open matches and tournaments into one clear place, so players can spend less time arranging and more time playing.

It is designed around the people who make local football happen: players, field owners and administrators.

01 / FOR PLAYERS

Make the next match happen.

Find a field, keep track of bookings and join games or tournaments.

02 / FOR FIELD OWNERS

Put your pitch on the map.

Share field details, publish open matches and organise tournaments.

03 / FOR ADMINS

Keep the game in order.

Manage the platform’s fields, accounts, bookings and competitions.
